Outcome and constraints

Two distinct faults sit behind one complaint. The buttons: HistoryModal and ChapterViewer render as siblings after the knot's own </Modal> (Knot.tsx:250-257) — a second modal opened from inside an already-open one, React Native's classic “nothing happens” failure. The clutter: the knot carries ~60 interactive controls across 11 components with six sections at identical visual weight, so the one thing touched daily (the cue) looks exactly like the thing touched once a year (account reset). Desired outcome: reading history opens, the way in looks like a control, and the sheet's first screen holds only what belongs to the daily ritual.

Constraints: offline, single-user, nothing leaves the device. No new dependency — everything here is React Native StyleSheet and existing primitives. §13.6 hard rules are untouched: no event written, no migration, no PRNG, no notification scheduling, no backup crypto, no partner hand-off. The existing log.write({ type: 'knot_open' }) keeps its exact semantics — it fires when the knot opens, never when a tier or group expands.

Scope and non-goals

In scope: nesting the two child modals and gating the pattern; the opener's resting affordance and live attention dot; the everyday/rare tier split with the rare tier grouped Your data · Practice · About; promoting an attention-needing section into the everyday tier; two glossary terms.

Non-goals: no change to what any section does — Safekeeping, Partner, Support, Adaptive and Reset are relocated, not rewritten. Reset keeps its hold-to-unravel gesture and 2500ms threshold exactly; moving a destructive control is not licence to touch its guard. No in-sheet drill-down rewrite (mockup option B/C, declined). No React Native Testing Library. Translation state/provider/copy stays out, owned by the parked knot-translation-switch plan.

Requirements (confirmed)

User stories

S01 — the repair (stories 1-2, 15) · S02 — the opener (3-4, 16-17) · S03 — the declutter (5-14, 18-21).

  1. As a reader with sealed days behind me, I want tapping “Reading history” to actually open the history list, so that I can revisit what I have read.
  2. As a reader browsing that history, I want tapping a chapter row to open the chapter, so that the list leads somewhere.
  3. As a reader on the reading screen, I want the way into the knot to look like a control, so that I do not have to guess that the faint text top-right is tappable.
  4. As a reader whose backup or support state needs attention, I want to see that on the reading screen, so that a problem with my data is not invisible until I happen to open the knot.
  5. As a reader opening the knot, I want the first screen to hold only the weave, my cue, and reading history, so that the thing I touch daily is not buried among things I touch once a year.
  6. As a reader wanting anything else, I want one clearly-labelled “More” row, so that there is exactly one place the rest lives.
  7. As a reader inside More, I want Safekeeping and Starting over grouped under “Your data”, so that the two things that can destroy or preserve my reading sit together.
  8. As a reader inside More, I want Partner and Adaptive policy grouped under “Practice”, so that the things shaping how I read sit together.
  9. As a reader inside More, I want the origin story and Support grouped under “About”, so that reference material is not mixed in with controls that change my data.
  10. As a reader who used to find the origin story, adaptive policy and account reset jumbled in one “App” section, I want each to have an obvious home, so that I stop hunting.
  11. As a reader whose Safekeeping needs attention, I want that section promoted into the everyday tier already open, so that a real problem is not hidden one level down.
  12. As a reader in that promoted state, I want the section to appear exactly once, so that I am not looking at the same controls in two places.
  13. As a reader whose attention state clears, I want the section to return to its group, so that the knot settles back to its short shape.
  14. As a reader who studies, I want the study library still reachable from the knot, so that moving it out of the everyday tier does not mean losing it.
  15. As a maintainer, I want a test that fails when any component opens a modal from inside its own open modal, so that this class of dead control cannot ship again.
  16. As a maintainer, I want the opener's attention check to be cheap and bounded, so that a control on the reading screen cannot repeat the launch-hang pattern.
  17. As a maintainer, I want the cheap probe pinned to the existing full check by test, so that two functions answering one question cannot drift apart.
  18. As a reader using a screen reader, I want each disclosure to keep announcing its expanded state and each promoted section its attention state in words, so that the dot is never the only carrier of meaning.
  19. As a reader with reduced motion on, I want the knot to keep honouring that setting, so that the tier split introduces no new animation I did not ask for.
  20. As a reader with a large OS font scale, I want group labels and rows to wrap rather than truncate, so that the deeper hierarchy stays readable.
  21. As a reader who reaches “Starting over” in its new home, I want the hold-to-unravel guard unchanged, so that relocating it has not made an irreversible action easier to trigger.

Out of Scope

  • Changing what any section does. Safekeeping, Partner, Support, Adaptive and Reset are relocated and regrouped, never rewritten — that keeps the diff reviewable and the blast radius at layout.
  • Reset's guard. The 2500ms hold, the reduced-motion/screen-reader two-tap fallback, and the Unravel animation are untouched. Moving a destructive control is not licence to touch its safety.
  • In-sheet drill-down and tabs (mockup options B and C). Both were live directions; A was chosen because it adds no navigation concept the app does not already have.
  • React Native Testing Library. Considered and declined by the owner — it would be real ongoing value but a new dependency and a chunk of work before any fix lands.
  • Translation controls. Owned by the parked knot-translation-switch plan; its carve-out is preserved.
  • The stale mockup-template.html palette. A real defect, but in the skill assets, not this feature. Noted for separate fixing.

Testing Decisions

  • Seam 1 — source invariants (test/ui-contracts.test.ts). New case: no component renders a modal-opening child after its own last </Modal>. Follows the file's established source-walking pattern (walk(SRC) + regex + named it), the same approach as test/boundaries.test.ts. It must be written to fail on the pre-fix tree first — a check that never went red proves nothing — and must assert its own component set is non-empty, since a regex matching nothing is how this rots into a vacuous pass.
  • Seam 2 — the diagnostics probe (test/diagnostics.test.ts). hasSupportAttention(db) must agree with needsAttention(getSupportSummary(db)) across clean, invariant-failed, snapshot-error, and recent-error-in/out-of-window fixtures. Follow that file's existing better-sqlite3 fake and injected-now patterns. The equivalence is the contract — it is the only thing stopping the cheap path from drifting from the real one.
  • Deliberately not tested: the tier layout, group ordering, promotion rendering, and the opener's appearance. The suite is Node-only with no component renderer (AGENTS.md), so any “test” of these would assert on source text, not behaviour. Named as an accepted gap rather than faked — the same honesty the existing it.todo font gap shows.
  • Not provable by Vitest at all: that the nested modals actually present on a device. By the owner's decision this is checked at release, not before merge.

Direction questions

Intent. The knot should feel like a short list with one door at the end, not a control panel. This is an extension of an existing pattern, not a new one: the same DisclosureSection, one level deeper, plus a group label the file already defines.

Hygiene. Exact values used: ink15 #E0DACB (opener border, rules), paper #F4F1E9 at ~72% (opener fill), ink40 #726D5C (resting dot, group labels, chevrons), madder #B5482F (lit dot), markSoft rgba(46,58,140,.08) (promoted-section ground), radius.pill 999, radius.panel 12, control.minTarget 44, space[3] 12 (nested indent). No theme switcher exists — one visual register only.

Details. Pressed → existing opacity .7. Empty → “Nothing sealed yet.” retained on the history row. Attention → dot plus “, needs attention” appended to the opener's accessible name, so the dot is never the sole carrier. Reduced motion → unchanged. Large font scale → group labels and row summaries wrap, never truncate. Focus → Close on open, opener on close, preserved through the opener restyle.
