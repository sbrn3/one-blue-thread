Outcome and constraints

Opening the app gets the reader to Scripture with less to read past first. The app moves like the Loom it already claims to be: threads draw, pull taut, pass and settle. Every existing animation works on a real phone, and the broken ones are fixed: the 5-second white launch screen, the seal hold that shows nothing until release, and layout jumps in the sheets.

Constraints:

  • Scripture-first voice.
  • One visual register: src/ui/tokens.ts, the Loom.
  • Motion intent is "tension and passage": no bounce, no spring, no fade-and-rise on everything, nothing performing (from the aesthetic-thread-textile/level-up.md motion intent).
  • Every animation honours the OS reduce-motion setting and runs on the UI thread with Reanimated.
  • No new dependency except expo-splash-screen, which the owner approved on 2026-10-08 (first-party and SDK-pinned). No new typeface. The italic face of the bundled Newsreader is the same family and is not a new typeface.
  • Synthetic data only in mockups, fixtures and committed screenshots. The owner's real data lives in the release app; on-device checks use only the dev app (com.sngugi.thread.dev).

Scope and non-goals

In scope:

  • Launch: a linen splash, no white gap, and the slow start-up work measured and moved off the first-frame path.
  • Hold gestures: the seal and the unravel show their progress from the moment a finger touches down.
  • Italic: the Newsreader italic is bundled and used at all 6 italic sites.
  • Sheet polish: the knot no longer jumps when it opens, the history and verse sheets slide consistently, and the verse sheet opens faster.
  • Motion foundations: motion tokens plus one reduced-motion hook.
  • The reading screen: the chosen direction's layout, order and arrival motion.
  • Seal: today's row woven by the shuttle on seal. The spec described this, but it was never built.
  • The knot: its opening becomes cloth.
  • Device review toolkit: committed scripts that record and tile phone frames, so any session can review the screen automatically.

Not in scope:

  • Onboarding visuals.
  • The shelf of finished bolts.
  • Changing what the lab measures. Sitting length is also excluded: James 4 is split into 11 sittings in the owner's data, which is worth a separate look.
  • Themes and dark mode.
  • Sound.

Requirements (draft, finalised after the direction gate)

User stories

  1. As the reader, I want the app to open onto linen and the weave with no white screen, so that opening it feels like arriving, not like loading. (F1)
  2. As the reader, I want the weave to cover whatever loading remains, and to step aside as soon as the reading is ready, so that a fast phone isn't slowed by it.
  3. As the reader, I want the seal line to start pulling taut the moment my finger touches, and the seal to land at 1.2 s without waiting for me to let go, so that I can feel the hold working. (F2)
  4. As the reader, I want releasing early to unwind the line visibly, with nothing logged except the existing hold_cancel, so that a cancelled hold reads as cancelled.
  5. As the reader, I want the unravel ring to come apart while held and re-weave if I let go early, with the same touch-down timing as the seal. (F3)
  6. As the reader, I want italic lines (my cue, prompts, notes) set in Newsreader italic, so that the scripture voice stays one typeface. (F9)
  7. As the reader, I want the knot to open without its contents jumping, so that it feels like one piece of cloth. (F4)
  8. As the reader, I want every sheet (history, memory, chapter, verse, dictionary) to enter and leave the same way, and a tapped verse to answer within about 150 ms. (F5, F6)
  9. As the reader on a busy day, I want much less to read past before Scripture, with the order the direction sets, so that the text comes first in spirit and in layout. (F10)
  10. As the reader on a quiet day, I want only the date, the chapter and Scripture, with no empty gap. (F10)
  11. As the reader, I want yesterday's probe offered before I read, still skippable in one tap, and for it to disappear once answered or skipped.
  12. As the reader, I want the memory passages due today offered before reading, capped by the daily cap as now.
  13. As the reader, I want What's new and the study hint to take one quiet line, or one card, for one day only, and never to sit between me and the text as a big box.
  14. As the reader, I want my cue echoed on arrival in the place the direction gives it, because the cue is what the product is built around.
  15. As the reader, I want to see today's row woven by the shuttle when I seal, and the fell line move down one row. (F8)
  16. As the reader, I want the knot's opening to look like cloth coming off the beam, at the same speed as today's slide.
  17. As a reader with reduce motion on, I want every one of these to appear in its end state at once, with no movement, and every hold to keep its tap fallback.
  18. As a reader with a large font size, I want the reorganised top of the screen to wrap without clipping, and every control to stay at least 44 pt.
  19. As a TalkBack user, I want the "before you read" items announced in order with their state (due, done), and the arrival to read date → chapter → cue.
  20. As the lab (silent actor), I want E11/E12 to keep hiding the day and sitting lines, E9 to keep its p=0.6 schedule and span, and E1's hold-or-tap arm to keep working, with any change in presentation recorded as a dated boundary.
  21. As the reader on a missed day or after a lapse, I want the lapse zone to keep its place before reading, inside the direction's "before you read" pattern.
  22. As a reader with no network, I want everything here to work offline, which it does: nothing in this plan touches the network.
  23. As a developer or agent, I want one command that records the phone during an action and tiles the frames into a single image, and one that taps elements by their label, so that screen reviews don't need hand-made coordinates.
