What this is deciding: what the reading screen shows before Scripture, in what order, and how the app moves. Each direction is drawn on the same busy day as the owner's screenshot. Yesterday's recall probe is due, two memory passages are due, and there is a What's new note. Next to it is the same direction on a quiet day with nothing due. The dashed red line is the bottom of the first screen on a typical phone.

Fixed in all three, because the lab depends on it:

  • Yesterday's probe stays before the reading. Asked afterwards, it would measure something else, and that would break experiment E9.
  • The day count and the sitting count can still be hidden by experiments E11/E12, and each layout still works without them.
  • The seal stays the fell line.

Press Play arrival under a phone to see how it opens. "Reduce motion" shows the end state with no movement, which is how the app behaves with the phone's reduce-motion setting on.

Direction A

Open at the text

Scripture starts on the first screen. The header becomes three short lines. Everything due before reading folds into one "Before you read" list. Yesterday’s probe (and a lapse, when there is one) starts open so the lab’s measurements stay clean; memory passages and What’s new stay folded until tapped. Chosen 2026-10-08.

GainsThe first verse is above the fold even on a busy day, so there's very little to read past before Scripture.
CostsThe cue line is smaller. On a probe day the open probe still sits above the text — shorter than today, but not zero.
Motion signatureOne weft draws under the title as you arrive. A tapped row opens with a running stitch. Text never fades in.

Direction B

The threshold

The first screen is one calm composition: this book's cloth, with today's row waiting for the shuttle, then the date, your cue and the chapter beneath it like a label. Anything due before reading comes after it one card at a time, and each card drops away once it's done. The clutter is handled by putting things in sequence, not by hiding them.

GainsIt's the most "you're arriving somewhere" of the three, and the cloth becomes the face of the app. The probe is unchanged apart from its frame, so E9 is barely affected.
CostsScripture starts one scroll down, on every day. Opening the app is a short ritual rather than the text itself.
Motion signatureThe warp threads drop into place, then the shuttle passes and leaves today's row ghosted. That row is woven for real when you seal. Cards leave with a weft pass.

Direction C

The margin

The thread rail widens into a margin, and everything that isn't Scripture hangs off it. The date and counts become small margin notes. Anything due is a coloured knot tied on the rail with a short tag, and tapping it opens a drawer beside the text. The main column is just the chapter title and the verses.

GainsThe text column is the cleanest of the three, and the rail stops being decoration: it holds the day's to-dos. It also scales well, since a third item is just a third knot.
CostsThe narrower text column means more line breaks. The margin type is small, so it needs care at large font sizes. The probe sits behind a tap, which is the same E9 note as A.
Motion signatureThe rail is alive: its threads settle with slight tension as you scroll, knots slide down to their place on arrival, and a done item's knot is pulled tight and vanishes.

All directions

Shared moments

These moments come with whichever direction you pick. Each one has the same rule: tension and passage. Threads draw, pull taut, pass and settle, with no bounce, no confetti and nothing performing. With reduce motion on, each one shows its end state at once.

Seal → today's row is woven

Today the seal tightens its line, and the weave below appears already finished. The proposal: on seal, the shuttle crosses the bolt and weaves today's row, and the fell line moves down one row. The spec has always described this ("today's thread animates in"), but it was never built.

The knot opens like cloth

Today the settings sheet slides up as a plain panel. The proposal: the sheet comes off the beam, so its top edge is a woven selvedge and its sections are drawn in by a single passing weft. It keeps the same timing as today's slide, so it's no slower.

Launch: no white screen

Today a cold start shows about 5 seconds of plain white before the weave. The fix: the splash is linen-coloured and stays up until the weave's first frame, and the slow start-up work is measured and moved off that path. The weave itself stays as it is.
