Book end — the prompt, now optional and multi-pick

DismissalZone today offers that book's marks and closes after one pick. Now each mark has its own "Learn this" (which turns to "Learning ✓"), and a single "Done" resolves the moment — pick none, one, or several. Marks not picked stay in the library's Marked list.
