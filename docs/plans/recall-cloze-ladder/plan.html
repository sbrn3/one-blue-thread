Outcome and constraints

Problem. Memorising a passage means being asked about specific verses and being helped up to recalling the whole thing. Today a due passage shows only a reference and then everything, and the next-day probe (E9) asks the reader to recall yesterday's whole chapter a day after one reading. That is too large to answer, and it read as a bug (#40).

Outcome. (1) A due memory passage becomes a cloze card: a few key words hidden at first, more over time, finally the whole passage hidden (#41), with letter stubs as the easier first step at each amount. (2) The next-day probe asks about a short, named span of verses the reader actually read, preferring a verse they marked (#40).

Constraints. Offline, single-user, nothing leaves the device. §13.6: events append-only, migrations additive-only, no Math.random(), /src/memory never imports /src/lab, and a recall grade can never touch seal, streak, weave or dose. Scripture-first voice: no generated prose. The Leitner scheduler (§21, "deliberately not Anki"), its fixed intervals and the 2-a-day cap are unchanged.

Scope and non-goals

In: cloze engine and ladder; a rung per passage; recall card UI; recording the verse range read at seal; the narrowed E9 probe and its UI; a guard so the dose analysis does not pool old and new probe grades. Non-goals: Anki-style scheduling or ease factors; typed answers; changing Leitner intervals or the cap; cloze on the probe; more than one promoted passage per book; non-English stop words.

Requirements (confirmed)

User stories

  1. As a reader with a due passage, I want the first reviews to hide only a few key words, so that I'm eased into recalling it.
  2. As a reader, I want the card to hide more as I review it more, ending with the whole passage hidden, so that it helps me memorise over time.
  3. As a reader, I want the first review at each amount to show the first letter of each hidden word and the next to show plain gaps, so that each step starts easier.
  4. As a reader, I want "Held it" to move me up, "Partly" to hold my place and "Lost it" to step me back one, so that the card follows how I'm doing without punishing a bad day.
  5. As a reader, I want Reveal to fill in the full text and then to self-grade with Held/Partly/Lost, and Skip to still work in one tap, so that nothing about answering changes.
  6. As a reader, I want the Leitner schedule, the 2-a-day cap and the "nothing due, no zone" behaviour unchanged, and I want a recall grade never to touch seal, streak, weave or dose.
  7. As a reader, I want the next-day probe to name specific verses with their reference, so that I'm never asked to recall a whole chapter.
  8. As a reader who marked a verse in yesterday's chapter, I want the probe to ask about that verse and say so, so that it asks about what I cared about.
  9. As a reader, I want the probe to ask only about verses I actually read (including a later sitting of a chapter), so that I'm never quizzed on text I never saw.
  10. As a reader, I want the same day always to give the same probe, and probes to fire on the same days as before, so that revisiting the screen can't re-roll it and the experiment is unchanged.
  11. As an existing user, I want my passages, boxes and due dates to survive the upgrade, and a well-learned passage not to restart from the easiest step, including after I restore a backup onto a fresh install, so that updating costs me nothing.
  12. As an existing user, I want the first day after upgrading (no stored read range) to simply have no probe, so that nothing breaks.
  13. As a screen-reader user, I want hidden words announced as "blank" (stubs with their first letter) and the ladder bars announced as "Step n of 4", so that the card is operable without sight.
  14. As an offline reader, I want the cloze card and probe to work from the active translation with no network, and a loading or failed text fetch to fall back to the plain reference card, so that recall never blocks my reading.
  15. As the lab (a silent actor), I want probe grades from before and after this change kept apart in the dose analysis, so that the recall outcome stays interpretable.
  16. As a reader who opened the app on the day I updated, I want that day's probe either to ask about specific verses or not to appear, never to show a broken prompt.

Out of Scope

  • Anki scheduling, ease factors, a larger daily queue: the grill kept §21.
  • Typing answers and automatic checking: the app does not judge the reader.
  • Cloze on the probe: it stays free recall so E9 measures the same kind of thing.
  • Part-of-speech tagging or non-English stop-word lists.
  • Letting the reader choose difficulty, or a peek-a-word interaction (Option B).

Verification

Automated (npm test + npm run typecheck, plus CI's APK build) is the real gate. Because this touches a schema migration and PRNG determinism, an itemised manual checklist is mandatory.

  • Upgrade a dev build that already has promoted passages in boxes 1, 3 and 5 → the app opens, nothing is lost, and their cards start at the matching step (not all at the easiest) and the rung is stored after the first grade.
  • Due passage, review 1 → a few key words hidden as stubs; Reveal fills them in; Held it advances the ladder bars at the next review.
  • Same passage, next review → the same words hidden as plain gaps; "Partly" keeps the bars; "Lost it" steps them back.
  • A passage at the top rung shows "Say it from memory" with no hidden-word text; Reveal then grades as usual.
  • Failure/retry: with ESV/NIV selected and an invalid key (or airplane mode), the card falls back to the reference-only view and recall still works.
  • Seal a sitting that is not the start of a chapter; next day the probe (when it fires) asks only about verses in that sitting.
  • Mark one verse in a chapter, seal, next day → the probe asks about that verse and shows "you marked this"; reopening the screen shows the same probe.
  • On the first day after upgrading, the probe does not fire and nothing crashes.
  • Restore a backup made before this update onto a fresh install → the passages open at the step matching their box, not all at the easiest.
  • Open the app on the update day before updating, then after → the probe either names verses you read or does not appear; it never shows "null".
  • Skip the recall and the probe in one tap each; seal, streak and weave are unaffected.
  • TalkBack/VoiceOver: the paragraph reads with each hidden word as "blank" (a stub says what letter it starts with); the bars read "Step n of 4".
  • Compare the built card to mockup.html#chosen and #probe on one narrow phone and one tablet, at large OS font scale.
