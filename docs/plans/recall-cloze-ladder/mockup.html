Chosen direction — A + C blend

Each amount of hiding is met twice: the first review at that amount shows letter stubs (the easier step), the next shows plain inline gaps. Reviews 1 and 2 hide the same few words, 3 and 4 about half, 5 and 6 nearly all; from review 7 the whole passage is hidden and there is nothing to stub. “Lost it” steps back one review position, so you meet the easier stubs again on the way back up.

Option A — Inline gaps

The verse stays set in Newsreader italic; each hidden key word becomes a ruled slot the same width as the word (like a worksheet blank). Nothing to learn: tap Reveal, the gaps fill in thread blue, grade yourself. Closest to today's card, so it adds the least new interaction.

Option B — Cloth strips (tap to peek)

Hidden words are small strips of undyed-then-dyed weft, matching the app's textile voice. Tapping one strip peeks that single word (a hint) without ending the attempt, so a half-remembered word can be checked cheaply. Adds a per-word interaction and a possible honesty question: does a peek cap the grade?

Option C — Letter stubs

Each hidden word keeps its first letter and dashes for the rest (“l— ”, “p—”). The strongest memory cue, so it suits the early rungs, but it makes the lowest rungs easier than A or B and leaks word length on every rung. It can't be used on the whole-passage rung, which falls back to a blank card.

Narrowed next-day probe (#40)

Today's probe says “Yesterday you read John 3. What do you remember?” and reveals the chapter. The narrowed probe names a short span with its reference. When you marked a verse in that chapter, it asks about that verse (shown with the same madder mark the reading screen uses for a kept verse); otherwise a seeded run of about three verses. Free recall and the four self-grades are unchanged.

Rungs shown: review 1 (about a fifth of key words hidden), review 3 (about half), review 5 (nearly all), review 7+ (whole passage). Click Reveal on any card. “Key words” here = any word not in a small English function-word list; which ones hide is a seeded spread, so a different review number hides a different set. Reduced-motion: the reveal is an instant swap (no animation is built, so reduced motion needs nothing).
