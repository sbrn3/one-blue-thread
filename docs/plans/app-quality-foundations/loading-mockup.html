The weft does the weaving.

The field begins as faint, wavy, unsupported warp across its whole height. An indigo weft is visibly drawn across the first row, then enters from the opposite side for the next. Only once a pass finishes does that row’s warp snap taut, its over-crossings appear, and the fell line step down to it.

Every reveal is scheduled from the same rowDone(j) the weft draw ends on, so tension is caused by weaving rather than by a mask sliding on its own clock. The paths use the app’s deterministic sine-wave geometry and alternating over/under crossings. On success the remaining passes complete quickly and the cloth dissolves into the reading; reduced motion presents the finished cloth immediately.
