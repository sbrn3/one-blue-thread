Problem & Intent

Status, owner & next action

Not yet approved. Owner: the user, who noticed ArrivalZone showing "James 2 · sitting 1 of 3" and "Day 15 in James" and asked whether this should be tested rather than assumed. Outcome and constraints

Answer, empirically and per-reader, whether the day-count line ("Day N in {Book}") and the sitting-count suffix ("· sitting X of Y") in ArrivalZone actually help the reader, using the app's existing on-device, zero-telemetry reversal-experiment engine (src/lab) — the same mechanism already used for E3 STREAK VISIBILITY — rather than resolving it by design judgment or building new experiment infrastructure. Constraint: single-user, offline, zero telemetry (AGENTS.md §Privacy, §20 "support with zero telemetry") — nothing about this reads or sends data off-device; the experiment's outcome (days sealed per phase) is computed and stored exactly like the four existing reversal experiments.

Scope and non-goals

In scope: two new reversal experiments, E11 (day-count visibility) and E12 (sitting-count visibility), appended to REVERSAL_QUEUE after E3; the conditional-render wiring in ArrivalZone.tsx/Flow.tsx; live per-phase visibility (not just the post-verdict applied setting).

Non-goals: fixing whatever gap E1/E3/ E4-floor may have around live per-phase enforcement (documented as a finding below, deliberately not touched here); reordering or otherwise changing E7/E4/E1/E3's existing queue position; a reader-facing settings toggle (considered and rejected in /grill — see grill-summary.md); de-emphasizing instead of fully hiding (rejected in /grill, round 3).

Dependencies and unresolved gates

None blocking. Both new experiments sit at the tail of the existing E7 → E4 → E1 → E3 queue, so no interaction with in-flight experiments. thread/ is currently clean on main (8e44b58) — confirmed via git status --short before this plan was written; no unrelated uncommitted work to preserve.

Requirements (confirmed)

User stories

  1. As the reader, when neither E11 nor E12 has an active Hidden phase and neither has concluded with a Hidden verdict, I want ArrivalZone to render exactly as it does today, so nothing changes for the ~11 months these two sit behind the existing queue.
  2. As the reader, during an active E11 phase whose arm is B (Hidden) and today's date falls inside that phase's window, I want the "Day N in {Book}" line fully omitted — not faded, not replaced.
  3. As the reader, during an active E12 Hidden phase, I want the "· sitting X of Y" suffix fully omitted, with the chapter line falling back to plain "{Book} {chapter}" exactly as it already does today when sittingsTotal === 1.
  4. As the reader, the two experiments' schedules are independent — E11 being in a Hidden phase must not affect E12's line, and vice versa.
  5. As the reader, once E11 or E12 concludes and a verdict is Applied (applyRecommendation), that arm governs permanently from then on — the same "applied settings, read fresh each render" contract E1/E4-floor/E3 already give the reader.
  6. As the lab engine, E11 and E12 must appear in REVERSAL_QUEUE, ARM_LABELS, RECOMMENDATION_TEMPLATES, and PROFILE_EFFECTS — every place the four existing reversal experiments are registered — so §13 "one reversal at a time" and the generic analysis/report code (renderReversalReport, applyRecommendation) treat them identically to E1/E3/E4/E7, with no silent fallback to generic "A"/"B" labels.
  7. As the owner reading an eventual Support-exported report, I want E11/E12's verdicts to render with real names ("DAY-COUNT VISIBILITY" / "SITTING-COUNT VISIBILITY") and real recommendation text, not the generic fallback test/report.test.ts:36-40 demonstrates for unknown ids.

  • Fixing E1/E3/E4-floor's apparent lack of a live per-phase check (see exec.md's design finding) — a pre-existing condition, not introduced or worsened by this plan, and fixing it risks disturbing three already-running/queued experiments' data. Flagged for a future session, not bundled here.
  • A reader-facing settings toggle for either line — considered in /grill and rejected in favor of the empirical route.
  • Any change to E7/E4/E1/E3's existing order, phase length, or PRNG mechanism (phases.ts/prng.ts untouched).
