Inside the app: onboarding presents Numbers 15:37–41 in full, followed by one factual context sentence. Any surface naming this passage or explaining the origin must contain the complete passage; constrained surfaces omit the origin entirely. Daily reading, recall, memory, and study preserve source text and attribution; there are no generated devotionals, summaries, “takeaways,” or spiritual interpretations.
