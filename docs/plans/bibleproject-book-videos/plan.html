Outcome and constraints

The reader wants context with a summary before and after reading a book, "to help consolidate things". That means two things:

  1. BibleProject's overview video, linked at a book's start and end.
  2. An optional one-line headnote per day, in the reader's own words, read back as that book's contents page when the book is finished and at any time in Reading history.

The README and the public site must also show the app as it ships today: its features, aesthetics, artwork and functionality.

Boundaries:

  • Scripture-first voice. The app never writes a headnote, and a headnote is never set in the Scripture face.
  • Nothing about headnotes is logged, and the lab never reads them (a new boundaries rule).
  • Migrations stay additive-only, and events is untouched.
  • Headnotes are in the encrypted backup and are wiped by the unravel.
  • Overviews are links, never embeds.
  • BRAND.md applies: no tassel or blue symbolism; the full Numbers 15 passage wherever it is cited; synthetic data only.

Scope and non-goals

In scope:

  • Overview links on Arrival (first sitting), at "You finished", and on the history book page.
  • Headnotes: write, edit and delete, with an optional verse range.
  • The contents page, per reading, at "You finished" and in Reading history.
  • Headnotes above the chapter in the chapter viewer.
  • Headpiece and tailpiece ornaments.
  • The README and website refresh.

Non-goals: see the Requirements tab, Out of scope.

Requirements (confirmed)

User stories

This list numbers the stories from 1. The two mockups count the group headings, so the same stories appear there as 2–28 (this list's 1–6 are their 2–7, 7–13 their 9–15, 14–20 their 17–23, and 21–24 their 25–28). exec.md uses this list's numbers.

  1. As a reader starting a book, I see BibleProject's overview link on my first sitting, so I get the book's shape before I read.
  2. As a reader of a two-part book, I see both parts, labelled, so I can watch the one for where I am.
  3. As a reader who has just finished, I see the overview again, after my contents, so I can watch it with the whole book fresh.
  4. As a reader, I can open any book's overview later from Reading history, so skipping it on day 1 loses nothing.
  5. As a reader whose link fails to open, I see the address to type, and nothing else breaks.
  6. As a reader of a book that shares a video (1–3 John, Ezra–Nehemiah, Kings, Chronicles), I get that video, honestly labelled.
  7. As a reader who has just sealed, I'm offered one headnote, never required to write one, so consolidating is a choice.
  8. As a reader writing a headnote, it belongs to the day's passage by default, and I can narrow it to specific verses in that chapter.
  9. As a reader, the field stays above the keyboard, holds 280 characters with a visible count, and can't be saved blank.
  10. As a reader who closes the sheet without saving, nothing is kept and nothing is logged.
  11. As a reader, I keep one headnote per day. If today's exists, Dismissal shows it and lets me edit it.
  12. As a reader who reopens later the same day, my headnote belongs to the passage I sealed, not tomorrow's chapter.
  13. As a reader with the app open past 4 AM, my headnote belongs to the day of the seal.
  14. As a reader finishing a book, I see this reading's contents: chapter numbers, my headnotes, dotted leaders and references, with today's appearing as soon as I save it.
  15. As a reader who wrote no headnotes, "You finished" shows no empty contents and no nudge.
  16. As a reader, I can open any book in Reading history, finished or in progress, and see its contents and overview.
  17. As a reader re-reading a book, each reading keeps its own dated contents, newest first, and "You finished" shows only this reading's.
  18. As a reader, tapping a contents row opens that chapter with my headnote above it and the verses highlighted.
  19. As a reader, I can edit a past headnote's words or verses, or delete it after confirming.
  20. As a reader whose licensed translation fails to load, I still see my contents, and the viewer offers Retry.
  21. As a reader, my headnotes are in my encrypted backup and come back on restore. Restoring an older backup replaces them, under the existing "overwrites everything" warning.
  22. As a reader who unravels the account, my headnotes are erased with everything else.
  23. As the lab (a silent actor), I never see headnotes: nothing is logged, and /src/lab cannot import them.
  24. As any reader, controls are 44pt and labelled; contents rows read as headnote, then reference; bare chapters read "no headnote"; ornaments are hidden from screen readers; it works at the largest font scale and under reduced motion.

Out of Scope

  • The relay (yesterday's headnote echoed at Arrival): deferred until the owner decides.
  • Embedding video, or any fetch from bibleproject.com: links only, which keeps the app offline-first.
  • Generated summaries or prompts, which would break the Scripture-first voice.
  • Cloth, dye or thread encoding in the contents rows, which round 4 rejected.
  • Restyling the Reading-history list itself, apart from the tappable section header. The shelf of finished bolts: separate work.
  • Changing the seal, the event log or isDismissalReady: the headnote is optional.
  • iOS and web builds: parked.

Verification

Automated (every slice): focused npx vitest run …, then npm test and npm run typecheck, plus CI's APK build.

Mandatory migration, backup and reset checklist, run on the dev client com.sngugi.thread.dev (APP_VARIANT=development), never on the release app:

  • Install the S02 build over an existing dev install at schema v13 → the app opens, Dismissal shows "A line for today?" after a seal, and nothing else changes
  • Seal, write a headnote and keep it → it shows in Dismissal; kill and reopen the app → it is still there, tied to today's sealed passage (not tomorrow's chapter)
  • Edit it, then save it blank → it is removed; open and close the sheet without saving → nothing changes
  • Type with the keyboard up → the field and Keep it stay visible
  • Export an encrypted backup, unravel, restore → the headnote is back
  • Restore a backup made before S02 → it restores cleanly with no headnotes, behind the existing "overwrites everything" warning
  • Unravel with headnotes present → after first run, no headnote remains anywhere (history contents are empty)
  • Failure: put the device in airplane mode and tap an overview → the address-to-type fallback shows and nothing else breaks

Visual (default): compare against mockup.html §5 on the dev build at one narrow phone width and one tablet width, with reduced motion on. Also check the largest font scale on the contents page (it is visually load-bearing).

Owner spot-check (optional): finish a short book (e.g. Jude) on the dev build and check that its contents read well. Read a long book's history contents (bare runs collapse). Then open the published site after S00 is merged.
