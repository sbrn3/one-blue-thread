Scroll the outlined phone with your mouse wheel or trackpad. Watch the rail on the left weave down as you read. When the seal line comes up the screen, the rail's fell meets it and the two become one line. Hold "Hold to seal" and today's thread runs out of the rail, across the page. Once it's sealed, the rail finishes weaving to the bottom. The other three phones are frozen at key moments for comparison.

The rule

  • While reading: the fell follows scroll progress, as it does now.
  • Once the seal line is on screen: the fell is whichever is higher, scroll progress or the seal line. The line can't sit below the woven cloth, and the thread always leaves the rail at the fell.
  • After sealing: the rail weaves the rest of the way down. Today is done.

Build notes
