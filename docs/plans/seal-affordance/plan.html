Outcome and constraints

The seal is the daily commitment and the gesture E1 measures, so it stays a 1.2 s hold that writes the same events. What changes is what the reader sees and touches. The end of the reading becomes a page-wide fell line: one loose thread running from the rail across the page, with an outlined "Hold to seal" pill on it. As the reader scrolls, the rail's fell locks onto that line, so the rail edge and the line are one continuous edge. Holding sends today's weft out of the rail and across the page. After sealing, the line stays blue as the chapter's end mark and the rail weaves down to the bottom.

Constraints: offline; no new dependency, font or icon; values from src/ui/tokens.ts; Reanimated worklets stay on the UI thread; reduced motion respected; Vitest is node-only, so only pure functions are tested; synthetic data only in artifacts.

Scope and non-goals

In scope: ThreadRail weaves its full height, is calmer, and never shows bare warp under cloth (S01). SealZone's hold path and its tap/screen-reader/reduced-motion fallback both become the line-and-pill row. ThreadRail's fell locks to the line and sweeps to the bottom after sealing (S02).

Non-goals: gesture timings, haptics and events; the two-tap link; anything in src/lab/; WeaveZone, DismissalZone and the knot's compact weave; per-book dye on the rail (M2 in level-up.md, a good follow-up); loom.ts.

Requirements (confirmed)

User stories

  1. As a reader, at the end of a reading I see an outlined "Hold to seal" pill on a thread across the page, so I know what to press.
  2. As a reader, when the seal line comes onto the screen, the rail's fell meets it and the two read as one edge, so the rail's progress leads me to the seal.
  3. As a reader, while the seal line is still off screen the rail follows my scroll progress exactly as it does now.
  4. As a reader, when I hold the pill, today's thread runs out of the rail and across the page over 1.2 s and the line pulls taut, with the same six haptic pulses, so I can feel and see the commitment.
  5. As a reader, if I let go early the thread pulls back over 220 ms and nothing is sealed (only hold_cancel is logged), as now.
  6. As a reader, if my finger drifts more than 20 px the hold cancels, as now, and the page doesn't scroll during the hold.
  7. As a reader, when the hold completes I feel the success haptic, the pill gives way to "Sealed · day N" on a blue line, and the rail weaves down to the bottom of the screen.
  8. As a reader who reopens the app later the same day, the line is already blue, the label shown and the rail full, with no animation replayed. The next day (after the 4 AM boundary) the line is loose again.
  9. As a reader who hasn't reached the floor yet, the pill reads "Read to the end to seal" (or "Start reading to seal") at reduced opacity and does nothing.
  10. As a reader using a screen reader, tap mode (E1 arm B) or reduced motion, I get the same line and pill; a single tap seals. Under reduced motion the line and rail change state without animating.
  11. As a reader, "Use two taps instead" still works, and sealing that way also turns the line blue.
  12. As a reader whose day count is hidden by the lab, the sealed label reads just "Sealed".
  13. As a reader, the rail weaves its full height and no longer stops short of its fell mark.
  14. As a reader, the rail is calm: threads don't cross, and loose warp never shows under woven cloth.
  15. As the lab (silent actor), the seal still writes the same events at the same moments, tokens.seal timings are unchanged, and the §11 mechanic-friction rung still reads hold_cancel the same way.
  16. As a reader with no network, everything works. Nothing here touches the network.
  17. As a reader on a very long chapter (176 verses), scrolling stays smooth: the new line is one SVG, and Flow's render cost stays inside its tested bound.
