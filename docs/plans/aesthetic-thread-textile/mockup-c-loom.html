the signature move · the fell line

Progress isn't a bar. It's an edge.

The fell line is where bare warp becomes cloth. Everything above it you've read; everything below is still under tension, waiting. It advances one pass a day — and it's the only progress indicator the app has.

reduced-motion: the cloth renders woven to its current row instantly — no shuttle animation

zone 05 · the weave, re-imagined

Every book you finish is a bolt of cloth.

Width is the book — one warp thread per chapter. Length is you — one weft pass per day you showed up. Psalms weaves a wide banner over months; Jude a narrow ribbon in an afternoon. Each book takes its own natural dye, so a year of reading is a shelf no one else could own. Gaps stay gaps — a skipped day leaves the warp bare, and the flaw stays in the cloth.
