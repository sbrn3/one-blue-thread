# One Blue Thread

Canonical product name: **One Blue Thread**  
Descriptor: **A quiet place to read Scripture.**

## The source of the name

The name comes from **Numbers 15:37–41**. The passage is presented before the
product metaphor. The factual context line is:

> This command was given to Israel: a blue cord on the tassels of their
> garments, visible as a call to remember and obey God’s commandments. One Blue
> Thread borrows that image; it does not replace the practice.

Do not call the app a digital tassel, treat the command as a generic Christian
mnemonic, claim the app fulfils it, or invent a symbolic meaning for blue. If
`tzitzit` or `tekhelet` is introduced later, define it accurately and obtain a
content review from someone competent in Jewish biblical practice.

### Full-passage rule

Any surface that cites Numbers 15 or explains the blue-thread origin contains
Numbers 15:37–41 in full on that same surface, with its translation and required
notice. There is no excerpt-only or link-only form. If a constrained surface
cannot hold the complete passage, it uses the brand name without citing or
explaining the origin.

## Scripture is the voice

One Blue Thread creates the conditions to arrive, read, remember, and return,
then becomes quiet. Content is ordered by authority:

1. Scripture, verbatim and attributed.
2. The reader’s own language: cue, remembered passage, and partner name.
3. Necessary system language: actions, consent, errors, recovery,
   accessibility, and factual behavioural reports.
4. Clearly labelled and attributed human commentary.
5. Factual product explanation and restrained marketing.

The app does not generate devotionals, passage summaries, prayers,
interpretations, spiritual diagnoses, “takeaways,” or simulated intimacy or
divine authority. Before adding prose, ask whether Scripture or the reader’s own
words can occupy that space instead.

Tyndale study resources remain optional, secondary, human-authored commentary
and retain their attribution. Validated SRBAI questions, factual experiment
reports, safety copy, consent, and instructions are not devotional prose and
remain when needed.

## Translation decision

The preferred brand-origin translation is **NIV 2011**. NIV wording must not be
committed until written permission or an applicable licence covers all intended
surfaces: bundled offline app code, public source repository, APK/GitHub Release,
GitHub Pages, screenshots and social/OG imagery, and future marketing reuse.
Record only the public-safe licence reference, edition, covered surfaces,
required notice, owner, term/expiry/revocation, and source-of-truth link here;
keep confidential agreements and credentials outside git.

Until then, the release source is the bundled **World English Bible (WEB)**,
which the repository already treats as public domain. The origin passage is
derived directly from `assets/bible/web.json`, not copied into a second source.

Current decision: **WEB release fallback; NIV permission pending.**

## Public brand versus compatibility identifiers

Change public display strings. Preserve these compatibility and implementation
identifiers across the rebrand:

- Android package `com.sngugi.thread`
- Expo slug `thread`
- SQLite filename `thread.db`
- SecureStore key `thread_backup_passphrase`
- existing event types, build SHAs, and deterministic fallback seed strings
- legacy `thread-backup-YYYY-MM-DD.json` import and cleanup support
- technical textile names such as `ThreadRail` and `tokens.color.thread`
- historical journal entries, shipped plans, tags, and git history

## Ownership gate

Public launch waits for a dated exact-name web/app-store search, relevant
trademark-register searches in intended jurisdictions (research, not legal
clearance), the canonical domain and key handle being secured, and a named
renewal owner. Availability snapshots are not ownership.

### Decision record — 2026-09-05

The exact phrase has unrelated descriptive and literary search results, but the
search found no competing Bible app or active software brand. The user accepted
those unrelated results and confirmed continuation with **One Blue Thread**.
This is a product decision, not trademark clearance. The canonical domain and
renewal owner remain unresolved and still block public launch.
