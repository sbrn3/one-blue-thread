What Thread asks you

Thread only ever watches one thing directly: whether you opened your Bible and how far you got. Everything else about how the habit is going — whether it stuck, whether you remember what you read — it has to ask. Three prompts do that.

Every answer below is self‑graded and consequence‑free. Grading a passage “lost” cannot touch your streak, cannot un‑seal a day, cannot shrink tomorrow's reading. The one exception is quiet and aggregate: probe grades feed a running score of which daily dose size actually gets remembered — never anything about the day you graded.

2 Memory‑verse recall · daily, up to 2 passages when due

The passages you chose to keep

Anything you've marked as a memory candidate and had promoted follows a Leitner box schedule — five boxes, at growing intervals — and comes back for recall exactly when it's due, never more often just because you're anxious about it.

3 SRBAI check‑in · monthly, once per calendar month

Has this become automatic?

The Self‑Report Behavioural Automaticity Index, reworded to measure initiation rather than the reading itself — whether opening the app has become automatic, independent of whether that particular day got read. It's the one place Thread asks instead of infers, because this one thing genuinely can't be read off the log.

one line, free text — the "formational line"
shown alongside the survey — "the monthly eyeball"

A plain list of every date Thread believes you read this month. Deliberately not a chart — a chart can be subtly wrong and still look plausible; a list of dates is checkable against your own memory in ten seconds.

Never re‑asked twice in the same calendar month, whatever you answer. The four scores average into one point on a monthly trend line — the only survey‑derived curve anywhere in the app.
related, but not a test

The lapse check‑in

After 4–7 days of silence, Thread has usually formed a guess about why — a changed cue, fatigue with the current book, a dose that's gotten too heavy, real life, or nothing sharp at all — and asks exactly one question matched to that guess. There's no score and no wrong answer; it's a nudge, not an instrument. It never fires inside the first 7 days of an unexplained total silence, and it never contacts your accountability partner without your tap.

  • "You used to read at the same point in your day; lately it's drifted, or stopped. Has something changed?"
  • "This book's been a slog lately, even though reading was going fine before it. Permission to stop here — that's allowed."
  • "You've been opening the app without finishing the reading. Today's ask just got smaller — no need to catch up."
  • "It's been quiet. No pressure — just checking in, once."
