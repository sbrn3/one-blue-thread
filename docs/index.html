World English Bible (WEB) · Public Domain

This command was given to Israel: a blue cord on the tassels of their garments, visible as a call to remember and obey God’s commandments. One Blue Thread borrows that image; it does not replace the practice.

the practice

Scripture is the voice.

One Blue Thread helps you arrive, read, remember, and return. It does not summarise the passage or manufacture a devotional around it. Once the text is before you, the app becomes quiet.

the lab · beneath the weave

how it learns

Small tests, run quietly — never on you.

An app with a million readers optimises for the average one. One Blue Thread has exactly one. It tests small changes in the reading routine and keeps only what helps you return to the text.

for example — one question, a few months

Weeks 1–5streak shown Weeks 6–10streak hidden Weeks 11–15streak shown Weeks 16–20streak hidden

For a few weeks your month shows a streak count. Then, without a word, it's gone. Then it's back. After a few rounds One Blue Thread can see whether that number actually helped you read — or just made a missed day sting more.

Whichever wins quietly becomes the new normal. Every change it makes, you can undo in one tap — it advises, it doesn't govern.
