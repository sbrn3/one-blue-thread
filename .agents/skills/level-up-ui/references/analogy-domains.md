# Analogy domains

For `analogy`. After stating the problem's structure in one sentence (actors, flows, bottleneck, no domain words), pick 3 to 5 domains whose *structure* rhymes. Mix at least one biological, one operational, one social. Transfer the mechanism, never the imagery. Drop any domain that needs a second forced role.

## Biological
- **Immune system** - distributed sensors, no central controller, escalation by chemical gradient, memory of past threats, tolerance of self. Good for: triage, alerting, false-positive load.
- **Forest mycelial network** - resource routing between unequal nodes, redirection around damage, slow background redistribution. Good for: uneven demand, sharing scarce capacity.
- **Coral reef** - structure built by many small identical organisms becomes shelter and substrate for a whole ecosystem. Good for: accretion, user-generated structure, platform effects.
- **Wound healing** - staged response (clot, inflame, rebuild, remodel), overshoot then prune, scar as permanent record of injury. Good for: incident recovery, versioning, audit trails.
- **Circadian entrainment** - internal clock, external cue ("zeitgeber") that resets it, jet lag when they diverge. Good for: sync problems, stale state, cross-timezone.

## Operational
- **Air-traffic control** - many independent movers, one congested channel, separation minima, handoff between sectors, flow control upstream of the jam. Good for: contention, routing, queueing.
- **Restaurant kitchen at rush** - mise en place before service, stations by station not by order, the pass as a single quality gate, the expediter calling the room. Good for: throughput, prep vs execute split, single checkpoint.
- **Hospital triage** - sort by acuity not arrival, reassess while waiting, fast-track for the trivial, a named owner per patient. Good for: prioritisation, dynamic re-ranking, SLA pressure.
- **Container shipping** - standard box, intermodal transfer without unpacking, ballast for stability, manifest travels with cargo. Good for: format portability, metadata that follows data.
- **Lock and weir on a canal** - move between levels in controlled steps, hold water back, meter flow. Good for: rate limiting, staged migration, backpressure.

## Social and cultural
- **Apprenticeship** - graduated trust, work alongside before working alone, the master's correction is the curriculum. Good for: onboarding, progressive disclosure, permission ramps.
- **Ship's log / watch handover** - append-only record, structured handoff at shift change, the log is the source of truth over memory. Good for: continuity across sessions, audit, on-call.
- **A debrief (aviation, surgery, military)** - blameless reconstruction, timeline first then causes, one improvement carried forward. Good for: retros, capturing deviations over compliance.
- **Potluck** - no central caterer, everyone brings, coordination just enough to avoid ten desserts. Good for: contribution models, light governance.
- **Town crier vs noticeboard** - push the urgent and transient, pin the durable, different channels by half-life of the message. Good for: notifications vs records.
