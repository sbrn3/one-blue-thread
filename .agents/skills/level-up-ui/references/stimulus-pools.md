# Stimulus pools

For `random-stimulus`. Draw 8 to 12 across at least five pools, no two adjacent from the same pool, at least two `[concrete]` and one `[abstract]`. Label each draw with its pool and kind. Track what has been used this session; draw fresh on a second batch. The value is the stimulus's *structural property*, not the image.
